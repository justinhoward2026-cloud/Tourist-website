Malacca City

Malacca is a Malaysian port city on the eastern shore of the eponymous straits, Malaysia's historic state, is a vibrant city with a rich history dating back to the 15th century. As such, it's filled with colonial landmarks and a hub for authentic Peranakan cuisine. just a few hours from Kuala Lumpur, Its history as a trading hub is reflected in its mix of architecture, from colonial Dutch buildings to Chinese shophouses and mosques. The city is also known for its vibrant food scene, with a variety of local and international cuisines to try.
